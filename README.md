A satirical, deeply relatable AI agent concept designed for developers and college students glued to their desks. Instead of tracking complex outdoor routes, this agent monitors how long you've been sitting in your hostel room or chair. When it detects hours of unbroken coding/scrolling, it uses an open-weight model (like Gemma) to generate a brutally honest, sarcastic, or comforting push notification forcing you to literally "touch grass"—giving you a quick, low-effort 5-minute outdoor micro-quest (e.g., "Go stand under a tree for 3 minutes, drink a glass of water, and look at the sky").
